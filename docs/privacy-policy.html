PuttPlug Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

PuttPlug does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The app works entirely offline. Everything you log stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. PuttPlug has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that gather data. The app does not request your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

Information you create

The putting sessions you record — dates, distances, makes, misses, and miss locations — are saved in the app's private storage on your device. This data is never uploaded anywhere. We have no servers and no way to see it.

Your data may be included in device backups you choose to make (for example iCloud Backup or Android Backup). Those backups are controlled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual sessions inside the app. Uninstalling PuttPlug permanently removes all app data from your device.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any data, because we never receive any. The app makes no network requests.

Children's privacy

PuttPlug is safe for users of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
