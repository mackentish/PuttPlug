PuttPlug

A putting tracker for disc golfers.

Log makes and misses at any range, see your real Circle 1, C1X, Circle 2 and Outside Circle percentages, and find out where on the basket your misses actually go.

Everything is stored on your phone. No account, no sign-in, and no network call of any kind — the app is fully usable standing on a practice field with no signal, which is the only place it matters.

  • Big MAKE and MISS buttons, each with its own −/+ to fix a miscount in place
  • Tap the basket to log a miss and where it hit, in one tap
  • 7-day, 30-day and all-time windows, with the change against the window before
  • A hotspot map of every miss you've marked
  • Your bag, with per-disc percentages
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs
